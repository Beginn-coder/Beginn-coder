### 2. Enterprise GRC & Third-Party Risk Assessment

A simulated enterprise Governance, Risk, and Compliance assessment evaluating a fictional cloud-based EdTech vendor against security control requirements and a defined organizational risk baseline.

The project demonstrates how to assess third-party security risks, document control gaps, prioritize findings, and develop remediation plans.

**Key areas:**
* Third-party risk management (TPRM).
* NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 control assessment.
* NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF).
* Risk identification and impact analysis.
* Plan of Action and Milestones (POA&M).
* Vendor security assessment and remediation tracking.

**Frameworks:** NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5, NIST SP 800-37 Rev. 2, and NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1.  
**Deliverables:** Vendor Security Assessment Report and POA&M tracking spreadsheet.

[View the GRC & Third-Party Risk Assessment repository]([https://github.com/Beginn-coder/Enterprise-GRC-Third-Party-Risk-Assessment-NIST-SP-800-53-RMF-])
